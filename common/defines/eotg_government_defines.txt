# (NGovernment, ~:550-573). RE-COPY THEM AFTER EVERY CK3 UPDATE (docs/pitfalls.md section 2) -
# a vanilla government added in a patch and missing here silently loses its modifiers.
# Precedent: After the End (workshop 3192256710) common/defines/ate_defines.txt:35-62.
