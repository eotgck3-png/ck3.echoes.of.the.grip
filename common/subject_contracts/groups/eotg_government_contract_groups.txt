# common/governments/eotg_<gov>_government.txt.
# Shape: feudal_vassal (G/common/subject_contracts/groups/

# levy tree (contracts/eotg_government_obligations.txt) plus the three generic

# coinage_rights (government_is_feudal content), succession_rights (feudal or
# tribal only), religious_rights, jizya_special_rights, kurultai_rights.
# Third obligations (board seat, enforcement, field commission) are G2.
