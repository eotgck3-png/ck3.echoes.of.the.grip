# Governments v2, Phase G1 (docs/specs/governments_v2.md §6.2): the 8 contracts,
# a tax tree and a levy tree per government.
#
# Shape: vanilla feudal_government_levies (G/common/subject_contracts/contracts/
# feudal.txt:128-190), copied for BOTH trees with fixed numbers: feudal's tax
# tree computes AI desire through government_is_feudal branches (:29-115) that
# never fire for these governments. subject_opinion, ai_liege_desire,
# ai_subject_desire and score are copied by position from the levy tree. Flags:
# obligation_high_levies (levy trees) and obligation_high_taxes (tax trees, as
# feudal_tax_high/_extortionate :71-121) at the high and extortionate levels;
# vanilla AI and opinion logic read them.
# Numbers are first-pass balance (§6.2 table); V-G12 is the check.
# Loc (localizer): <contract>, <contract>_<level>, <contract>_<level>_short,
# <contract>_<level>_desc (_subject_contracts.info "Text").
# -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
