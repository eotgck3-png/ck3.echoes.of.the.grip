After many years of research, journeys across planes, and compiling
testimonies from hundreds of people, it is with great pride that I
present this comprehensive history of the Titans and the material planes
during the First Era. This work could not have been completed without
the collaboration of countless individuals and the discovery of
long-forgotten documents. Each trip to the planes, each encounter with
the surviving relics of this era, has been an honor and a reminder of
the immense power that shaped our reality. The First Era was a time of
both creation and destruction, a saga of two Titans locked in an eternal
struggle for dominion. It is my sincere hope that this book brings
clarity to the chaos of that age and stands as a testament to the
perseverance of those who survived. Dedication: This work is dedicated
to all who shared their knowledge, to the brave souls who ventured
alongside me, and to the lost civilizations whose stories shaped our

powerful enough to end the eternal conflict with Orrin once and for all.

power from the stars themselves. What Yu did not realize, however, was

allowed Carrigore to escape Yu's mind while still subtly influencing

paranoia. The blade became a symbol of Yu's descent into tyranny, as he
became consumed with the idea of ultimate control. The sons of
Yu---Elross, Bohemut, and Malvrick---recognized that their father was no
longer the figure of balance he once was and plotted to overthrow him.

Orrin, leading Yu to prepare for a final battle. In a climactic
confrontation, Yu, blinded by his obsession and trusting in his sons,
was caught off guard when Bohemut turned Light's Edge against him. The
sword cut Yu from eye to eye, blinding him and leaving him forever
