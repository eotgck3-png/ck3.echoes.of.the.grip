## 1. Executive Summary & Core Paradigm

### The Scale Reality
In *Echoes of the Grip*, map hierarchy scales beyond vanilla medieval geography:
* **Barony = Star System / Anchor (or sub-cluster)**
* **County (Province) = Region spanning several to several dozen stars**

Because a single province encompasses an entire constellation or stellar pocket, representing a holding as a single physical building, solitary planet, or medieval town creates an irreconcilable scale mismatch.

### The Visual Metaphor: "The Command Center Holotable"
The CK3 3D map is framed as an **in-universe tactical holographic star table** projected in a fleet bridge or planetary war room:
1. **Terrain:** The procedural starfield lattice, dust lanes, and nebulae generated by `pdxterrain.shader` ([`docs/map_terrains_and_shaders.md`](file:///c:/Users/river/Documents/GitHub/ck3.echoes.of.the.grip/docs/map_terrains_and_shaders.md)) form the astrogation chart.
2. **Provinces:** Tactical jurisdictional boundaries across the stellar cluster.
3. **Holdings:** **Tactical Holographic Beacons / Transponder Relays**, not physical miniature structures.
4. **Armies:** Retained as **humanoid tactical avatars** (representing the commanding admiral, void-knights, or expeditionary marine corps deployed to the sector), deferring custom unit rigging to cultural passes later.

This paradigm completely resolves the scale issue and caps the 3D asset budget to **exactly 7 low-poly geometric silhouettes**.

## 2. Holding Taxonomy & Conceptual Mechanics

### 2.1 The Distributed Infrastructure Web
A holding is not a single point of habitation; it represents the **dominant institutional network projecting sovereignty across the stars in that province**:

| Engine Key | Base Mod Name | Sector-Scale Reality | Government-Specific Variations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `castle_holding` | **Bastion** | **Sector Defense Grid & Command**<br>Interdiction relays, naval staging, fortified garrison choke-points. | **PMC:** Taskforce Staging / Fleet Base<br>**Cartel:** Enforcer Den / Iron Ring<br>**Imperial:** Star Hold / Bastion |
| `city_holding` | **Port** | **Commercial Transit Conduit**<br>Hyperlane junctions, freight routes, corporate free-ports, mining exchanges. | **Corporation:** Corporate Enclave / Directorate Hub<br>**Gob-Corp:** Trade League Basin / Market Lattice<br>**Cartel:** Shadow Port / Smugglers' Run |
| `church_holding` | **Sanctum** | **Monastic & Spiritual Circuit**<br>Observatories, pilgrim way-stations, Titan reliquaries, archive spires. | **Void Reverence:** Event-Horizon Observatory<br>**Titan Cults:** Titan Forge-Reliquary<br>**Secular:** Central Data Spindle |
| `tribal_holding` | **Clanhold** | **Autonomous Pioneer Fringe**<br>Dispersed asteroid settlements, frontier havens, unaligned outposts. | **Frontier:** Frontier Haven / Pioneer Drift<br>**Raiders:** Warband Drift / Scrap Hold |

### 2.2 Mobile Holdings (Nomads & Herders)
Vanilla CK3 1.20 nomadic, pastoralist, and camp mechanics place assets on the map via `00_nomad_buildings.txt`:
* **Nomads (`nomad`) $\rightarrow$ Mobile Flotilla Anchor:** An unanchored, drifting warband battlegroup roaming the star pocket.
* **Herders (`herder`) $\rightarrow$ Mobile Harvester Fleet:** Dispersed resource-siphoning operations and nomadic convoys.

## 3. 3D Map Asset Pipeline & Technical Specifications

The asset pipeline is already codified in [`docs/marker_model_brief.md`](file:///c:/Users/river/Documents/GitHub/ck3.echoes.of.the.grip/docs/marker_model_brief.md) and [`docs/tools/build_marker_assets.py`](file:///c:/Users/river/Documents/GitHub/ck3.echoes.of.the.grip/docs/tools/build_marker_assets.py).

### 3.1 Strict Budget: Exactly 7 Meshes
To prevent endless 3D authoring cycles and high compute costs, **no tier-upgrades, cultural variants, or detailed textures will be modeled for holdings**. Upgrades are reflected in the 2D UI.

The complete mesh roster required in `gfx/models/buildings/eotg_markers/`:

```
eotg_marker_castle.obj          -> Fortress Shield / Aegis Diamond (Angled defensive fins)
eotg_marker_city.obj            -> Concentric Torus / Gateway Ring (Segmented halo)
eotg_marker_temple.obj          -> Ascending Obelisk / Crystal Spire (Vertical faceted needle)
eotg_marker_tribal.obj          -> Spiked Chevron / Crossed Wedges (Aggressive scavenger silhouette)
eotg_marker_nomad.obj           -> Swept Delta / Flotilla Arrowhead (Mobile fleet rally read)
eotg_marker_herder.obj          -> Tri-Point Satellite Hub (Dispersed harvester beacon)
eotg_marker_temple_citadel.obj  -> Crowned Spire (Sanctum obelisk inside Bastion shield)
```

### 3.2 Technical Mesh Rules (Per Marker Brief)
1. **Silhouette Over Surface:** The engine applies `EOTG_MARKER_EMISSIVE 0.82` flat emissive shading. Normal maps and fine surface details are completely discarded by the shader. Spend polygons only on external silhouette.
2. **Triangle Budget:** Target $\le 1,500$ triangles per mesh. Hard ceiling $2,000$.
3. **Plinth & Beam:** Every emblem sits on the shared procedural plinth (`eotg_marker.mesh`) and receives an auto-fitted vertical light projector beam generated by `build_marker_assets.py`.
4. **Orientation:** Y-up, centered on $(X=0, Z=0)$, triangulated faces only (no quads), outward counter-clockwise winding.
5. **Suppression of Medieval Artifacts:** Vanilla walls and ground dirt decals are neutralized by pointing `99_background_graphics_buildings.txt` to `western_walls_00_mesh` (handled by `build_marker_assets.py`).

### 3.3 Army Pawns Decision
* **Humanoid Models Preserved:** Armies remain 3D animated character pawns.
* **In-Universe Read:** Tactical holographic commander avatars / marine strike divisions.
* **Implementation:** Zero custom rigging needed now; custom armor/cybernetic variants deferred to cultural graphics passes (`unit_gfx`).

## 4. Internal Holding Structure (Province-Wide Infrastructure)

Building slots in holdings represent **macro-scale civilizational projects** across the constellation:

1. **System Logistics & Transit:**
   * *Hyper-Buoy Relay Network:* Inter-system transit speed, supply limit, reinforcement rate.
   * *Automated Freight Lattice:* Province tax multiplier and development growth.
2. **Resource Extraction & Industry:**
   * *Asteroid Strip-Mining Convoys:* Heavy raw tax income, minor control penalty.
   * *Cybernetics Fabrication Complexes:* Drives augmentation supply/events ([`cybernetics_seller_names.txt`](file:///c:/Users/river/Documents/GitHub/ck3.echoes.of.the.grip/cybernetics_seller_names.txt)).
   * *Planetary Biosphere Seeding:* Habitation expansion, increasing levy pool and tax base.
3. **Security & Fleet Defense:**
   * *Deep-Space Listening Posts & Interdiction Grids:* Fort level and hostile raid attrition.
   * *Flotilla Maintenance Slipways:* Men-at-Arms maintenance reduction.
4. **Megastructure / Landmark Slots (Duchy / Sector Capitals):**
   * *Precursor Conduit / Gate Anchor:* Sector-wide development and logistical nexus.
   * *Titan Relic Siphon:* Arcane power, prestige, or faith authority.
