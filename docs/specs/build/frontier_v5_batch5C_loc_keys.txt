frontier_v5 batch 5C: loc keys for eotg-localizer
=================================================

Source: docs/specs/frontier_v5.md §3.4 (#1 round 1), §7 (5C), §8 (lore constraints).
Script: events/eotg_frontier_events.txt (.050-.056 at the end of the file; .005
desc_dispute); common/scripted_effects/eotg_frontier_effects.txt (cause chain).
eotg-scripter, 2026-10-12, uncommitted.
File: localization/english/eotg_frontier_l_english.yml (UTF-8 BOM, Canadian
English, bare [x.GetName], no dashes, no numbers for progress or strain).

Voice (event_quality_v1 §12.1): main desc first person, present tense. Every
desc_* variant is an appended fragment ("\n\n..."), neutral or third person, and
still carries at least one I, me or my (rule 13). At most one NPC line per desc,
in unescaped straight double quotes (§12.2), true for every character the scope
can resolve to (rule 14). Options are first person or imperative (rule 8).
Short .tt lines may use the existing .002.ease_tt / .002.push_tt register.

Glossary: Region (county), System (barony), Frontier, backer, strain, settlers,
crews, berths. Never "pressure", "charter", "mayor", "bishop", "village".
Never hard-code "knight" (use Custom('KnightCulture...') if soldiers must be
named; "soldiers" is fine).

Banned in all round-1 text (spec §8, §10 item 5): plague, pestilence, miasma,
buboes, leeches, human, humanity, charter, Void, Orrin, Orro (as fact),
tribunal, arbiter, court (as an outside court), League, Archive, implant
rejection, self-repairing machinery, any named faith, god, culture, species
or group (Laughing Blades, Carrigore, Black Wyrms). Never [rite|E].

Hidden-value rule: progress and strain are words only, never numbers.

Lore voice samples (spec §3.4.3, from eotg-lore-keeper; use as the register
anchor, not necessarily verbatim):
  .050 "The scrubbers on the lower decks failed a month ago, and now the coughing has spread to every berth."
  .051 "The families who were here first have stopped trading with us, and last night they cut the beacon's power line."
  .052 "Half the habitat ring has lost pressure, and the crews say the frame itself is cracking."
  .053 "We are patching seals with seals we already patched once."
  .054 "[eotg_frontier_founder.GetName] says the Systems were built with [eotg_frontier_founder.GetHerHis] sweat, not my coin."
  .055 "The settlers will not share a chapel with the crews who came with the second convoy."
  .056 "The newcomers keep their own berths, their own songs and their own grudges."
(Note: "lost pressure" in .052 is a physical fact about the habitat and is fine;
the glossary ban on "pressure" is for the strain value.)

Reused keys (already defined; no new text): eotg_frontier.002.ease_tt,
eotg_frontier.002.push_tt.

Scopes available in every round-1 event: root (the holder), the Region as
eotg_frontier_county ([eotg_frontier_county.GetName]), and, when the Region
has a founder, eotg_frontier_founder (saved in immediate; may be root in
.050-.053 and .055-.056, never root in .054). Do not use the founder scope in
.050-.053, .055 or .056 text: it may be absent or be root.

Shared: the new strain cause "dispute"
----------------------------------------------------------------------

eotg_frontier.005.desc_dispute
  Appended to .005 (The Frontier Falters; strain at the failure point) when
  the latest strain came from a dispute (.054.d, .055.d, .056.d). Shape and
  length of the siblings (.005.desc_hardship, .005.desc_danger): starts
  "\n\n", first person, past tense. Must convey: the settlers' quarrels over
  who owns, worships or bunks where wore the venture down; the split was
  never mended. No outside authority, no faith or culture named.

eotg_frontier_band_cause_dispute_tt
  One line in the Invest/Build/Abandon legibility tooltip, shown only when
  strain is high and the latest cause is a dispute. Sibling register:
  "Hard choices have worn the settlers down." / "Raiders and worse prey on a
  weakly held Region." Must convey: quarrels among the settlers are dividing
  the work. Neutral or second person, as the siblings are (the existing
  band lines use "Your" once).

eotg_frontier.050 Sickness in the Berths (bg clinic; strain cause hardship)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Path: the yearly flavour roll, any running Frontier of mine. Lore §8 item 1:
habitat sickness (bad air from failing scrubbers, fouled recycled water,
radiation) or a fever in crowded berths. Use: sickness, fever, scrubbers,
quarantine berth, medic.

eotg_frontier.050.t
  Title. Intent: "Sickness in the Berths".
eotg_frontier.050.desc
  First person, present. Sickness is spreading through the settlers' berths
  from failing life support. Voice sample .050.
eotg_frontier.050.desc_hostile
  Shown when the Region is Hostile. Appended fragment: the Region's air (or
  radiation) is part of the cause; the Region itself is working against us.
  Carries I/me/my.
eotg_frontier.050.a  [gold]
  "Seal a quarantine berth and pay the medics." Costs gold; the work slows a
  little.
eotg_frontier.050.a_tt
  The sick are isolated and treated, and the work slows while they recover.
  No number.
eotg_frontier.050.b  [education_learning; no icon, skill icon only]
  "Trace it to the scrubbers myself." Must read as competence (knowing how to
  find the fault). Uses .002.ease_tt.
eotg_frontier.050.c  [ungated]
  "Keep the crews working." The work moves faster and the settlers suffer for
  it. Uses .002.push_tt. Stress: compassionate gain, callous loss.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Path: the yearly roll, any running Frontier. Lore §8 item 2: the people
already living in the Region ("a few hardy families"), or raiders working out
of it. No named group, no species, no native species.

eotg_frontier.051.t
  Title. Intent: "The Ones Who Were Here First".
eotg_frontier.051.desc
  First person, present. Those already in the Region have turned on the camp:
  trade stopped, sabotage. Voice sample .051.
eotg_frontier.051.desc_dangerous
  Shown when the Region is known to be Dangerous. Appended fragment: I knew
  the Region was dangerous, and this is part of it. Carries I/me/my.
eotg_frontier.051.a  [gold]
  "Trade with them, not orders." Costs gold. Uses .002.ease_tt.
eotg_frontier.051.b  [forgiving; trait icon]
  "Forget the cut line; offer them berths in the camp." (No semicolon if the
  house style prefers; no dash.)
eotg_frontier.051.b_tt
  They are taken in rather than driven off: the strain eases and the camp
  gains hands. Words only.
eotg_frontier.051.c  [duchy or above; no icon; costs prestige]
  "Send my own soldiers to hold the line." Must read as authority. Never
  "knights".
eotg_frontier.051.c_tt
  My own soldiers guard the camp: the strain eases and the work goes on
  safely. Words only.
eotg_frontier.051.d  [ungated]
  "Guard the line and keep building." Uses .002.push_tt. Stress: wrathful
  loss, compassionate gain.

eotg_frontier.052 The Frame Is Cracking (bg field camp; cause hardship)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Path: the yearly roll, a Frontier with some work done (progress at least a
fifth). Lore §8 item 3: structural failure (seals giving, a section losing
pressure, the frame cracking, a station going dark). NO self-repairing
machinery, no ecological collapse.

eotg_frontier.052.t
  Title. Intent: "The Frame Is Cracking".
eotg_frontier.052.desc
  First person, present. A section of the habitat is failing. Voice sample
  .052.
eotg_frontier.052.desc_late
  Shown when the Frontier is well advanced (two thirds or more). Appended
  fragment: the oldest sections, built fastest at the start, are the ones
  giving way. Carries I/me/my.
eotg_frontier.052.a  [Region check only]
  "Evacuate the section and rebuild it right."
eotg_frontier.052.a_tt
  Shared by a and b. The section is rebuilt properly: real work is lost, but
  the settlers feel safer (strain eases). Words only.
eotg_frontier.052.b  [diligent; trait icon]
  "I will walk the frame with the crews myself." Less work lost than a. Uses
  .052.a_tt.
eotg_frontier.052.c  [education_stewardship; no icon]
  "Salvage the frame and reroute the crews." Competence (managing materials
  and crews).
eotg_frontier.052.c_tt
  Most of the frame is saved: a little work is lost, nothing else. Words only.
eotg_frontier.052.d  [ungated]
  "Shore it up and keep the crews inside." Uses .002.push_tt. Stress:
  compassionate gain (medium), callous loss.

eotg_frontier.054 Whose Systems Are These? (bg council; cause dispute)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Path: the yearly roll; the Frontier has a valid founder who is NOT me, and the
work is at least half done. Right portrait: the founder. Lore §8 item 5 and
LAW AT 866: the founder (possibly a Local Ruler, my vassal) presses for more
under MY law. No court, arbiter, tribunal or outside authority.

eotg_frontier.054.t
  Title. Intent: "Whose Systems Are These?"
eotg_frontier.054.desc
  First person, present. The founder argues that the work, and so the
  Systems, are theirs. The desc's ONE NPC line is the founder's (voice sample
  .054; use [eotg_frontier_founder.GetName], GetSheHe, GetHerHis). True for
  any founder: assume no kinship, rank or gender beyond "my founder".
eotg_frontier.054.desc_ruler
  Shown when the founder already stands to hold the new System as Local
  Ruler under me (a city or church project; A1 founder first). Appended
  fragment: under my own arrangement they would already govern it, under me;
  this is about how much more. Carries I/me/my. No promise of ownership.
eotg_frontier.054.a  [founder is landless; Settlement, Trade or Religious project]
  "The System will be theirs to hold, under me."
eotg_frontier.054.a_tt
  CONDITIONAL (spec text constraint): the founder is pleased and the strain
  eases; the System will be [eotg_frontier_founder.GetHerHis] to hold under
  me "if [eotg_frontier_founder.GetSheHe] still leads it when the work is
  done". Must NOT promise it outright.
eotg_frontier.054.b  [gold]
  "A purse, and back to work."
eotg_frontier.054.b_tt
  The founder is paid and pleased, and the work picks up. Words only (the
  gold amount shows as vanilla effect text).
eotg_frontier.054.c  [arbitrary; trait icon]
  "What I gave, I can give to another."
eotg_frontier.054.c_tt
  The founder resents it, but the settlers fall in line (strain eases).
  Dread shows as vanilla effect text.
eotg_frontier.054.d  [ungated]
  "The coin was mine, and so is the Region."
eotg_frontier.054.d_tt
  The founder resents it and the quarrel festers: strain grows from the
  dispute. Stress: humble gain, arrogant loss.

eotg_frontier.055 Two Chapels (bg council; cause dispute)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Path: the yearly roll, when the settlers do not share one faith: the Region's
faith differs from mine, or a valid founder's faith differs from the
Region's. Lore §8 item 6: scoped faiths only, never a name; zeal is rising.
No gods, no Orrin cults, no Void. "Chapel" is the word.

eotg_frontier.055.t
  Title. Intent: "Two Chapels".
eotg_frontier.055.desc
  First person, present. Settlers of different faiths will not share a
  chapel. Voice sample .055.
eotg_frontier.055.desc_founder
  Shown (first) when the founder's faith differs from the settlers'. Appended
  fragment: the one leading the work does not share the settlers' faith.
  Third person about the founder is fine, but do NOT use the founder scope
  ([eotg_frontier_founder...]) here unless guarded; plain "the founder" is
  safe. Carries I/me/my.
eotg_frontier.055.desc_county
  Shown otherwise (the Region's faith differs from mine). Appended fragment:
  the settlers keep a faith that is not mine. Carries I/me/my. May use
  [eotg_frontier_county.GetName]. No faith names, no [faith|E] links needed.
eotg_frontier.055.a  [Region check only]
  "One chapel, shared by rota."
eotg_frontier.055.a_tt
  The settlers share by turns: tempers cool (strain eases), but the work
  slows a little. Words only.
eotg_frontier.055.b  [education_learning; no icon]
  "Hear both sides and write the rota myself." Competence (learning,
  theology). Uses .002.ease_tt.
eotg_frontier.055.c  [cynical; trait icon; costs gold]
  "Build two chapels and end the argument."
eotg_frontier.055.c_tt
  Two chapels end the quarrel: the strain eases markedly. Words only.
eotg_frontier.055.d  [ungated]
  "My faith keeps the chapel."
eotg_frontier.055.d_tt
  My faith holds the chapel; the others resent it and the dispute adds to
