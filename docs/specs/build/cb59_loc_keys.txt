Register (binding, §12.1 rule 7): log entries. Never I, me, my, we, our, and
never you or your. "Logged", "filed", "Next item" style. Not "the implant's
voice", no second speaker. Each key is shown INSTEAD of its default twin (not
in addition), to a Seamless (eotg_total_integration) reader only. Keep the
default's facts and any [..GetName] it carries; change only the register.
Fragments keep the leading "\n\n" of their default twin.
