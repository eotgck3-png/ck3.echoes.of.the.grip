----------------------------------------------------------------------
5A: #6 (.005 "Lead it yourself") and #4 (fascination)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

eotg_frontier.005.e
  Option name. Intent: "Lead it myself." Shown when the Frontier has failed
  (strain at the failure point), the founder is someone other than me, and I am
  an adult and free. Available in every .005 cause variant (no_founder,
  low_control, occupied, war, sponsor_lapsed, danger, hardship); read it against
  each desc. Effect truths: I become the founder; half the work stands; strain
  drops to the middle (not gone); I take stress (my own time).

eotg_frontier.005.lead_tt
  Tooltip. Must convey: I take charge of the venture myself, half the work
  stands, and the strain eases. MUST NOT say the System will be mine: under A1
  a city or church System still goes to a Local Ruler (the founder cannot be
  the holder), so promise nothing about who holds it. The stress cost shows as
  vanilla effect text; do not restate it.

eotg_frontier_research_fascination_tt
  custom_tooltip text wrapping add_fascination_progress (holder's culture).
  Shape: vanilla cathedral_fascination_progress_tt
  (pam_great_project_types_l_english.yml:130): "Your [culture|E] gains ...
  progress towards its current [cultural_fascination|E]". Shown only when the
  holder's culture has a fascination. The amount VARIES (10, or 15 when the
  Region has a Research Facility, Ancient Ruins or Anomalous), so do NOT
  hard-code "5%" or any single number. Either say it in words ("gains
  progress towards") or, if you can verify a working dynamic form for a
  temporary scope value (scope:eotg_frontier_fascination), use that; the spec
  allows a percent here because fascination is not a Frontier hidden value.
  The context is a completed Research Frontier (shown in .004 option tooltips).
  The no-fascination fallback is vanilla lifestyle XP text; no key needed.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
5B: #2 trait-gated options (14 names, 9 tooltips)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Personality options show the vanilla trait icon; education and tier options
show none, so the text must read as competence (education) or authority
(tier) on its own.

.002 (desc variants: low/mid/high strain, each with optional hostile/remote/
dangerous paragraph; g and h need a valid founder, so never Without a Founder)

eotg_frontier.002.g   [patient]
  "Slow the work and wait the year out." Strain eases a little; the work
  loses a little ground.
eotg_frontier.002.g_tt
  Must convey both halves: the strain eases, and the work slips back
  somewhat. No numbers.
eotg_frontier.002.h   [education_stewardship]
  "Recut the ration ledger." Costs a trifling sum (tiny gold, shown by
  vanilla); strain eases. Reuses eotg_frontier.002.ease_tt.

.003 (letter from the prospective backer; desc plus optional desc_replace
when a backer already exists)

eotg_frontier.003.c   [education_intrigue]
  "Accept, once I know what they would rather keep quiet." Same as .003.a
  (they become the backer, the first shipment arrives) plus strain eases.
  Must read sensibly whether or not desc_replace shows (an old backer is
  replaced in that case). Root's reply to a letter: first person. Reuses
  .003.accept_tt and .002.ease_tt (both shown).

.005 (all cause variants)

eotg_frontier.005.f   [duchy or above; prestige cost]
  "Order my vassals' crews in." Authority, not competence. Costs prestige
  (vanilla text).
eotg_frontier.005.f_tt
  Must convey: crews from my vassals take up the work, the strain falls
  well back from the breaking point (to a moderate level, not to nothing),
  and none of the work is lost. No numbers.

.030 (letter from the rival backer; one desc)

eotg_frontier.030.c   [education_diplomacy]
  "Let each backer hear what the other offered." I keep my current backer.
eotg_frontier.030.c_tt
  Must convey: [eotg_frontier_sponsor.GetName] stays my backer and is
  pleased; the strain eases; the work moves forward a little. The rival
  gains nothing. No claim or say for anyone (LAW AT 866).

.031 (one desc)

eotg_frontier.031.d   [deceitful]
  "Promise them a say, and let the promise lapse." Backer pleased; work
  moves forward a little. Reuses eotg_frontier.031.no_say_tt (the backer
  gains no claim and no say), which stays true: the promise is empty.

.032 (desc_settlers only: e requires no liege overlord)

eotg_frontier.032.e   [duchy or above]
  "Remind them whose Region this is." Authority. Dread gain shows as vanilla
  text.
eotg_frontier.032.e_tt
  Must convey: the settlers back down, the strain eases, but the work loses
  a little ground. No numbers.

.033 (one desc; the founder and settlers quarrel)

eotg_frontier.033.d   [impatient]
  "Both camps work double shifts until it is done." Reuses
  eotg_frontier.002.push_tt (faster work, settlers pay for it). True: large
  progress gain, strain rises (hardship).

.034 (desc plus optional Dangerous or Remote paragraph; check both)

eotg_frontier.034.d   [education_martial]
  "Send an armed party to recover what can be saved." Competence.
eotg_frontier.034.d_tt
  Must convey: part of the lost run is recovered; the work loses only a
  little ground and the settlers are spared any extra strain. Must fit a
  Dangerous Region (hostiles) and a Remote one (distance). No numbers.
eotg_frontier.034.e   [temperate]
  "Cut every ration, mine first."
eotg_frontier.034.e_tt
  Must convey: the work keeps its pace (no ground lost), but the strain on
  the settlers rises. No numbers.

.035 (ruins variant only: d requires flag:ruins)

eotg_frontier.035.d   [education_learning]
  "Catalogue it before anyone touches it." Reuses eotg_frontier.035.a_tt per
  spec. WARNING: the current .035.a_tt text says the work "loses some
  ground, unless the Frontier is a research venture". On .035.d the work
  GAINS a little on a non-research Frontier (+1) and more on a research one
  (+4). The reused tooltip is false on the non-research path. Localizer:
  either rephrase .035.a_tt so it does not state a direction for
  non-research (it must stay true for .035.a, which does lose ground), or
  request a new key eotg_frontier.035.d_tt from the scripter (one-line swap).

.036 (one desc)

eotg_frontier.036.c   [content]
  "Take what the crews need, and leave the rest."
eotg_frontier.036.c_tt
  Must convey: the work moves forward a little and the strain eases; the
  seam is not surveyed, so the Region gains no Rich Resources trait. No
  numbers.

.040 (desc or desc_military; d requires a company; never desc_none)

eotg_frontier.040.d   [education_martial]
  "Drill the settlers and hold it ourselves." Competence. Must fit both the
  Dangerous (escort offer) and Military-no-backer (backing offer) variants.
eotg_frontier.040.d_tt
  Must convey: no fee to the company and no escort; the strain eases; the
  drilling costs the work a little ground. The captain gains nothing. No
  numbers.

.041 (desc_head or desc_order; d requires a backer, so never desc_none)

eotg_frontier.041.d   [zealous]
  "Lead the first rite there myself." Piety gain shows as vanilla text.
  needs-lore 3 (spec §8): whether "rite" collides with the 1.20 rite system
  word is open; use "rite" only if the lore-keeper clears it, else e.g.
  "service".
eotg_frontier.041.d_tt
  Must convey: the strain eases and the work moves forward a little. The
  backer gains no claim and no say (may be restated). No faith or god named.
  No numbers.
