## A8.1: the worded bands (eotg_frontier_legibility_tooltip_effect) on the Invest, Build and Abandon
## decisions. Words only: never a number, a percentage or "N years". Each is one line in a list of up
## to four (pace, time, strain, cause), so keep them short and parallel.
## Pace line: the Region's yearly Establishment gain.
eotg_frontier_band_stalled_tt => The work has stalled: nothing is moving it forward. (Gain under 1 a year. The time line is suppressed, so this line must stand alone and imply "no end in sight".)
eotg_frontier_band_pace_slow_tt => The work goes slowly. (Gain low: below the typical unaided project.)
eotg_frontier_band_pace_steady_tt => The work goes at a steady pace. (A typical project with no backer.)
eotg_frontier_band_pace_fast_tt => The work goes quickly. (Typically a sponsored project.)
## Time line: how much work remains at this pace. Not shown when stalled, or once the work is done (the
## existing waiting line, below, takes its place).
eotg_frontier_band_time_decade_tt => About a decade of work remains. (8 or more years at this pace; "about a decade" is a phrase, never "8 years".)
eotg_frontier_band_time_years_tt => Some years of work remain. (3 to 7 years at this pace.)
eotg_frontier_band_time_near_tt => The work is nearly done. (Under 3 years at this pace.)
## Strain line (.002's bands: 4 and 6; .005 "the Frontier falters" fires at 8).
eotg_frontier_band_strain_calm_tt => The settlers are holding up. (Low strain.)
eotg_frontier_band_strain_strained_tt => The settlers are under strain. (Middling strain; nothing fails yet.)
eotg_frontier_band_strain_breaking_tt => The settlers are near breaking point; the project could falter. (High strain, close to the failure event.)
## Cause line: what weighs on it most recently (var:eotg_frontier_last_cause), shown only with the
## strained or breaking line. Say the cause, not a remedy the script doesn't offer.
eotg_frontier_band_cause_no_founder_tt => Nobody is leading the work. (The founder is dead, a child, imprisoned or gone from your realm; Appoint a Founder fixes it.)
eotg_frontier_band_cause_low_control_tt => Your hold on the Region is weak. (Low county control.)
eotg_frontier_band_cause_occupied_tt => The Region is occupied. (An enemy holds its capital.)
eotg_frontier_band_cause_war_tt => War has reached the Region. (A war you lead, or hostile armies in the Region; since batch 1 a distant ally's war does not count.)
eotg_frontier_band_cause_sponsor_lapsed_tt => The backer's money has stopped. (The sponsor withdrew, died or could not pay.)
eotg_frontier_band_cause_danger_tt => Raiders and worse prey on a weakly held Region. (Dangerous trait, control below the project's floor, no Garrison Post, escort or Military project.)
eotg_frontier_band_cause_hardship_tt => Hard choices have worn the settlers down. (Strain from event options and hard years.)
