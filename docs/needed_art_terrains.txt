ECHOES OF THE GRIP — NEEDED ART: TERRAIN TYPES
================================================
Covers all 14 reflavoured terrain types.

Each terrain entry lists the three art layers CK3 uses:

  MAP TEXTURE  — the visual appearance of the terrain on the 3D province map.
                 Files live in gfx/map/terrain/ (shared with vanilla; mod versions
                 go in gfx/map/terrain/ under the mod root to override).
                 These are large seamless DDS tiles — typically DXT1 or DXT3.

  TERRAIN ICON — the small UI icon shown in the province summary and tooltips.
                 Files live in gfx/interface/icons/terrain_types/
                 Size: 40x40 px (match vanilla), DXT5 format.

  BATTLE BG    — the widescreen background shown during battles in this terrain.
                 Files live in gfx/interface/illustrations/battle_backgrounds/
                 Size: 1920x540 px (match vanilla), DXT1 format.
                 NOTE: Battle backgrounds are expensive to produce. Lower priority
                 than map textures and icons; vanilla placeholders are acceptable
                 for v1 if the terrain icon and map texture are done.

Priority order for production: TERRAIN ICON first, MAP TEXTURE second, BATTLE BG last.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PLAINS → "Open Transit Expanse"
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TERRAIN ICON:  A flat expanse of space with faint warp-lane grid lines — open,
               empty, traversable. Pale blue-grey on dark void.

MAP TEXTURE:   Deep space with a subtle grid or warp-lane overlay. Should read
               as "open and fast-moving" compared to cluttered terrain types.
               Dark base with faint luminous corridor lines.

BATTLE BG:    Open void engagement — two fleets at long range, stars visible,
               no cover or obstacles. Feels exposed and fast.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HILLS → "Asteroid Belt Region"
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TERRAIN ICON:  A cluster of irregular rocky asteroids, varying sizes, dense but
               not impenetrable. Mid-tones of grey and brown.

MAP TEXTURE:   Rocky asteroid field — irregular boulder shapes scattered at mid
               density. Not so dense it reads as Debris Cluster. More open than
               forest terrain, less cluttered than jungle.

BATTLE BG:    Fleet engagement threading through a sparse asteroid field —
               cover available but not dominant. Rocks in foreground, fleet
               visible through gaps.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
MOUNTAINS → "Fortified Sector"
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TERRAIN ICON:  A hardened military installation or fortified orbital platform —
               angular, defensive, blockade-shaped. Distinct from the open station
               of Stable Relay Nexus.

MAP TEXTURE:   Dense fortification geometry — interconnected defensive platforms,
               blocky station structures, hardened transit chokepoints. Dark metal
               and grey. Should feel impassable.

BATTLE BG:    Battle in a defended installation corridor — tight fighting space,
               blast doors, fortified choke points. Claustrophobic, high ground
               advantage implied for the defender.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DESERT → "Sparse Dead Zone"
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TERRAIN ICON:  A dead star or burned-out system — empty orbit rings, cold and
               featureless. No infrastructure visible.

MAP TEXTURE:   True empty space — minimal stars, no structure, no landmarks. The
               colour palette should be cold and desaturated compared to Open
               Transit Expanse. Should feel barren and isolated.

BATTLE BG:    Engagement in empty dead space — no cover, poor visibility,
               distant cold star the only reference point. Eerie and featureless.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DESERT MOUNTAINS → "Fortified Dead Zone"
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TERRAIN ICON:  A hardened installation in a dead-system context — defensive
               geometry but surrounded by cold empty space. Combines the
               isolation of Sparse Dead Zone with the density of Fortified Sector.

MAP TEXTURE:   Fortified structures in dead space — the platforms of Fortified
               Sector but with the cold empty-space palette of Sparse Dead Zone.
               Should feel like a defended outpost in the middle of nowhere.

BATTLE BG:    Defensive engagement at a remote hardened outpost — limited cover,
               cold void beyond the walls, survival aesthetic.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
JUNGLE → "Dense Drift Zone"
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TERRAIN ICON:  Overlapping drift debris and asteroid fragments in a chaotic jumble
               — dense and visually confusing. Differs from Debris Cluster in that
               drift zones imply active movement and instability rather than
               static wreckage.

MAP TEXTURE:   Very dense, chaotic drift region — overlapping debris clouds, gas
               pockets, unstable masses. The busiest, most cluttered terrain texture.
               Warm-tinted nebula gas mixed with rocky fragments.

BATTLE BG:    Engagement inside a dense drift cloud — poor visibility, debris
               striking hulls, disorienting environment. High chaos, low range.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FOREST → "Debris Cluster"
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TERRAIN ICON:  Scattered hull wreckage and large shattered sections of destroyed
               ships or stations — recognisable as artificial debris, not natural
               rock. Differs from Asteroid Belt Region in its manufactured origin.

MAP TEXTURE:   Orbital graveyard — large hull sections, broken station rings,
               scattered small debris between them. Cold metal tones. Should
               feel like the aftermath of a battle or collapse event.

BATTLE BG:    Fight in a wreck field — broken superstructures providing cover,
               debris hazards, claustrophobic but not as dense as Dense Drift Zone.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
OASIS → "Stable Relay Nexus"
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TERRAIN ICON:  A functioning relay station or trade hub — geometric, well-lit,
               with visible antenna arrays and docking arms. Suggests activity
               and stability. Should feel welcoming compared to all other terrain icons.

MAP TEXTURE:   This terrain should appear as a distinct point-feature rather than
               a tile — a bright station node against neutral space. If CK3 handles
               oasis as a small feature, match that scale. Warm lighting, active.

BATTLE BG:    Defence of a functioning station — interior corridors or the station
               exterior with docking arms visible. Higher stakes implied; this is
               infrastructure worth fighting for.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TAIGA → "Cryo Frontier"
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TERRAIN ICON:  A cryo-sealed habitat dome or failing thermal installation — frost
               crystallisation visible on surfaces, blue-white cold tones. The
               space equivalent of a frozen frontier.

MAP TEXTURE:   Ice-world orbit or cryogenic frontier region — blue-white cold
               palette, frost crystallisation on structures, dim failing light
               sources. Should feel dangerously cold and isolated.

BATTLE BG:    Combat on or near a cryo-world — frozen surfaces, failing thermal
               systems, ice formations obscuring sight lines. Cold palette,
               equipment failures implied.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WETLANDS → "Flooded System Expanse"
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TERRAIN ICON:  A partially submerged or flooded planetary system — water-world
               aesthetics from orbit, blue-green, with infrastructure half-visible
               beneath water or behind flood clouds.

MAP TEXTURE:   Flood-world orbital band — dense water vapour, blue-green atmospheric
               haze, submerged or partially flooded structures visible below.
               Should evoke the Xerxes catastrophe: a world swallowed by water.

BATTLE BG:    Engagement above or near a flooded world — storm cloud cover, water
               vapour reducing visibility, a half-submerged structure in background.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEPPE → "Longlane Expanse"
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TERRAIN ICON:  A long unobstructed warp lane — a single bright corridor line
               stretching into the distance, suggesting fast travel but exposure.
               Differs from Open Transit Expanse in that the lane itself is the
               feature, not just open space.

MAP TEXTURE:   Wide open expanse with faint warp-lane markers — similar palette
               to Open Transit Expanse but with slightly more structure (lane
               markers, distant waypoints). Fast-travel aesthetic. Pale and open.

BATTLE BG:    Engagement along a warp lane — long engagement distances, no
               terrain cover, high mobility implied. Feels like a highway ambush.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FARMLANDS → "Core Trade Corridor"
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TERRAIN ICON:  A well-maintained transit corridor with active traffic — multiple
               vessel silhouettes, cargo transports, bright and busy. The most
               productive and infrastructure-rich terrain icon.

MAP TEXTURE:   Densely developed transit corridor — station clusters, beacon
               arrays, heavy traffic lanes. Warm and active tones. The richest
               terrain texture, suggesting high development and economic value.

BATTLE BG:    Battle over a major trade corridor — cargo vessels caught in
               crossfire, station infrastructure in background, high economic
               stakes visible. Should feel consequential.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FLOODPLAINS → "Resource-Rich Corridor"
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TERRAIN ICON:  An active resource extraction corridor — mining platforms, ore
               haulers, processing facilities visible. Rich but slightly less
               developed than Core Trade Corridor.

MAP TEXTURE:   Resource extraction zone — mining platforms in foreground, ore
               clouds or gas extraction rigs, industrial warm tones. Productive
               but rougher than Core Trade Corridor. More industrial, less polished.

BATTLE BG:    Fight over a resource extraction site — mining platforms providing
               cover, ore clouds obscuring visibility, industrial infrastructure
               at risk.


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DRYLANDS → "Failing Frontier Sector"
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TERRAIN ICON:  A deteriorating frontier installation — damaged or half-functional
               station, failing power systems suggested by dim lighting, crumbling
               infrastructure. Between Sparse Dead Zone (empty) and normal
               habitation (functional).

MAP TEXTURE:   Dilapidated frontier region — damaged structures, power failures
               visible as dark sections of what used to be lit installations, cold
               desaturated palette. Should feel like a region in decline,
               not yet fully dead.

BATTLE BG:    Combat in a failing installation — flickering lights, structural
               damage, crumbling cover. Dangerous environment as much from
               infrastructure failure as from the enemy.

PRODUCTION PRIORITIES
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Priority 1 — Terrain Icons (40x40 DXT5)
  All 14 icons are needed before the mod can ship without placeholder confusion.
  These are small and fast to produce. Start here.

Priority 2 — Map Textures (seamless DDS tiles)
  Most visible to players. Produce in this order:
  1. Open Transit Expanse (plains — the most common terrain)
  2. Core Trade Corridor (farmlands — economically significant)
  3. Debris Cluster (forest — common in helix/remnant regions)
  4. Asteroid Belt Region (hills — second most common open terrain)
  5. Remaining types in any order.

Priority 3 — Battle Backgrounds (1920x540 DXT1)
  Lowest priority. Vanilla placeholders will not crash the game.
  Produce these only after icons and map textures are complete.
  Most impactful to produce first: Core Trade Corridor, Fortified Sector, Dense Drift Zone.
